## D-005 — OpenCV fisheye calibration and undistortion as mandatory pre-step

Date: 2026-09-04
Decision: Undistort all JetBot video frames with OpenCV before further processing (cv2.fisheye.undistortImage, balance=0.1). One-time calibration of the IMX219-160 lens with checkerboard pattern (cv2.fisheye.calibrate).
Rationale: The 160° fisheye camera of the JetBot is directly compatible with no downstream tool — Brush accepts only PINHOLE, MASt3R was trained on pinhole images. Undistortion with balance=0.1 produces sharp ~105° rectilinear images without black borders, compatible with the entire pipeline.
Consequences: Python + OpenCV as dependency in preprocessing. One-time calibration effort with printed checkerboard pattern after JetBot assembly. Effective FOV reduces from 160° to ~105° — still wide enough for indoor spaces.
