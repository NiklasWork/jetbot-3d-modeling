## D-013 — Stop-and-Go with script-controlled capture

Date: 2026-09-04
Closes: OD-002
Decision: Images are created in stop-and-go: the human steers the direction, a script on the robot drives a bit, stops, waits out the oscillation, takes a shot and repeats.
Rationale: Image sharpness is the input variable on which the whole pipeline depends — the rolling shutter camera delivers blurry and sheared images during the drive in indoor lighting, and neither pose estimation nor training can repair that. The script needs no ML and makes the operation simpler than a video drive, not harder.
Consequences: The capture script belongs in repos/robot. Images are already downscaled on the robot to at most 1280×960 — reason is transmission time and COLMAP runtime, not Brush: it downscales itself via `--max-resolution` (verified on 2026-09-04). But **in color** — grayscale saves only 20–30 % with JPEG, brings COLMAP nothing (converts internally to gray anyway) and costs the model its color. The lever is the resolution, not the color channel. ffmpeg is no longer needed in normal operation.
