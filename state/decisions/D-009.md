## D-009 — COLMAP as pose estimator

Date: 2026-09-04
Supersedes: D-003
Closes: OD-001
Decision: Camera poses are estimated with COLMAP (Sequential Matcher); MASt3R and the MLX port are omitted without replacement.
Rationale: Human requirement "only proven projects" (state/profile.md). COLMAP: 12,647 stars, actively maintained, de facto standard of photogrammetry. The previously planned mlx-mast3r had 11 stars, one fork and no license — below the maturity level that D-002 cited as a reason for rejecting msplat. Additionally regarding content: a video run with high overlap is the most favorable case for COLMAP's Sequential Matcher, and COLMAP delivers the initial point cloud that Brush expects — with MASt3R a custom-written converter would have had to generate it.
Consequences: `brew install colmap` as dependency. Slower than a feed-forward estimator, but without custom build between pose and training. Risk remains: textureless white walls: capture (light, loops, viewing angles) helps against this, not tool choice.
