## D-014 — Semi-autonomous manoeuvres as the driving algorithm

Date: 2026-09-09
Decision: The human picks the standpoint, the robot drives the capture manoeuvre — orbit, wall pass, panorama — itself; coverage and loop closure stay human, and closing the loop on the wall pass with a trained regressor is a later, separate step (context/plan-driving.md).
Rationale: It gives the robot what this hardware is good at, repeatable short moves with settle-and-shoot discipline, and leaves the human what nothing without odometry or a map can supply. It needs no training to start and adds one Python file to shared hardware.
Consequences: context/plan-driving.md is no longer gated on an open question — packages 1–3 are the built path, 4–6 stay a deliberate later entry. D-006 is not superseded: manual control remains the outer loop and this is the expansion it names. What is overtaken is only the timing in D-006's consequence clause and in OD-003's own "needed from human" — the choice was made on the human's instruction before the vertical slice ran, so packages 2 and 3 are now gated on hardware rather than on a decision.
Closes: OD-003
Rejected: B autonomous room loop — a segmentation mask is not a distance to the wall, and it still could not tell when a room is covered; C trained reflex avoider — it learns both parallax degeneracies at once and its dataset cost returns with every room; D fixed open-loop patterns — drifts without bound and is only honest in a cleared area; E nothing — leaves the capture geometry at the level of the human's keyboard technique.
