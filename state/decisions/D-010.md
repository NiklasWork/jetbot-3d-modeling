## D-010 — Undistortion via COLMAP instead of checkerboard calibration

Date: 2026-09-04
Supersedes: D-005
Decision: The fisheye distortion is estimated by COLMAP from the shots themselves (camera model `OPENCV_FISHEYE`) and converted into pinhole images with `colmap image_undistorter`; no separate checkerboard calibration.
Rationale: With COLMAP as pose estimator (D-009) the reason for the manual pre-step falls away — the undistortion is part of the tool, is estimated from the real shots instead of a separate calibration session, and can no longer be invalidated by a differing sensor mode.
Consequences: No OpenCV dependency in preprocessing, no printed checkerboard, no calibration appointment. Fallback remains open: if COLMAP estimates the distortion poorly at 160°, a checkerboard calibration can be submitted later as a fixed specification — then as a new decision, not silently.
