What the three status colours mean

Built and measured Written, never run on hardware Open

The counts are real: pipeline-3d 5 of 6 packages done and measured, robot 0 of 5 ever executed on the device, driving 1 of 6 built against a stand-in robot. Every figure in the strip comes from context/measurements.md.

Mixing is allowed

These are directions, not packages. “C's grid with A's dark ground”, “B's type on A's palette” are legitimate answers — as is “none of these, but warmer”.
